# Editing the Desert Grey Website

Hey — you don't need to know how to code to update the website. You also don't need to remember a password.

## How to log in

1. Go to **https://desertgreymusicfestival.com/admin**
2. Type your email
3. Click **Send login link**
4. Open your email — there'll be a message from Desert Grey with a magenta button. Click it.
5. You're in.

The login link expires after 15 minutes and only works once, so it's safe even if someone else sees it in your inbox.

Once you're in, you'll stay logged in on that browser for a week.

## What you can edit

Everything on the public site that's text, an image, or a video. The editor on the left side groups it the same way the site is laid out:

- **⚙️  Site Settings** — festival name, date, ticket URL, Spotify link
- **🔔  Announcement Bar** — the pink strip at the top
- **🎬  Hero** — the first big screen with the video background
- **🧭  Top Navigation** — the menu at the top of the page
- **📜  Marquee** — the scrolling text bars (genres, dates)
- **💬  About / Intro** — the "festival carved out of dust" block
- **🎸  Lineup** — band names in three tiers (headliners, main bill, support)
- **🕐  Schedule** — the running order with stages and set times
- **🎫  Tickets** — the three pricing tiers
- **🤝  Partners** — sponsor names
- **🎧  Listen** — the Spotify section
- **📍  Venue** — Nile Theater info, address, map, travel
- **❓  FAQ** — questions and answers
- **🦶  Footer** — sponsors, columns, copyright

Click any section in the left sidebar to start editing.

## How to make a change

1. Click the section you want to edit.
2. The fields appear on the right. Each one is labeled in plain English ("Hero Headline", "Ticket Price", etc.).
3. Edit any field — text, dates, links, images.
4. Click **Publish** in the top-right.
5. The site updates in about 30 seconds. Refresh the public site in a different tab to see your change.

## Uploading images and videos

When you see an **image** or **video** field, click it. A media browser opens. You can:

- **Upload new** — drag and drop any image or video onto the browser.
- **Use existing** — pick something already uploaded.

Recommended sizes:
- **Hero video** — MP4, ideally under 10 MB. Looped, so 10–20 seconds is enough.
- **Photos** — JPG or PNG, no wider than 2400 pixels.
- **Sponsor logos** — PNG with transparent background works best.

The system handles resizing for mobile automatically.

## Lists (bands, sponsors, FAQ questions, etc.)

When a section has a list (like the lineup):

- **Add** — click the **+** button at the bottom of the list.
- **Remove** — hover an item and click the trash icon.
- **Reorder** — drag the dots on the left of each item up or down.

## A few things that need slight care

**Headlines with highlighted words.** Some headlines (Hero, Intro, Schedule, etc.) have a "Headline Emphasis" field. Whatever you type there will appear in magenta italics in the headline. The word must appear *exactly* as written in the main headline — so if your headline is `Lock in your pass.` and your emphasis is `your pass.`, the word "your pass." in the headline will be styled. Match the spelling and punctuation.

**Stage IDs.** In the Schedule section, each stage has an "ID" field (`nile`, `underground`, `coffee`). The site uses these to color-code each stage. Don't rename existing ones — if you want a new color scheme, ask Diego.

**Links.** When you see a link field:
- `#tickets` or `#lineup` means "jump to that section on the same page"
- `https://something.com` means "go to an external website"

**The countdown.** The countdown timer at the top reads from the **Festival Date** in *Site Settings*. Update it once there and the rest of the site follows.

## What if I break something?

You can't actually break it. Every edit creates a save point — if something looks wrong, message Diego and he can revert to the previous version with one click. The original is never lost.

## Adding new team members

If you want to give someone else editing access, send Diego their email. He'll add them to the allowlist and they can log in the same way you do — no signup needed.

## Logging out

You don't need to. Sessions auto-expire after a week. But if you want to, there's a **Logout** button in the top-right of the editor.
